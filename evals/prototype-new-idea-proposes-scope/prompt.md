/plotwork:prototype

My partner and I run a community garden with about 30 plots. When people go on holiday their plots dry out. I want to try an app where plot holders ask the others to water for them while they're away, but before we plan the whole thing I need to see if people actually cover for each other. It must let people sign up with their plot number, and post "I'm away these dates, who can water?". Later we might add a seed swap, booking the tool shed, a compost rota, and collecting the yearly plot fee online. We keep our projects in Azure DevOps.
