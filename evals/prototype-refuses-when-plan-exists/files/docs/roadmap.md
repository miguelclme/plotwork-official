# Book Club Hub — Roadmap (approved)

**Vision:** make it easy for small book clubs to organise meetings and pick books.

**Goals**

1. Run a club without spreadsheets.
2. More members join the discussions.
3. Works well on phones.

**Users:** organisers and members.

**Out of scope:** selling books, video calls.

## Phase 1 — MVP "Run a club"

- Create a club.
- Invite members by link.
- Schedule meetings.
- Propose and vote on the next book.

## Phase 2 — Engagement

- Discussion threads per book: members post and reply; the organiser can pin a post.
- Reading progress: members mark chapters as read; the club sees overall progress.

## Phase 3 — Polish & performance

- Pages load in under 2 s on 4G.
- Accessibility audit (WCAG 2.1 AA) before the public launch.
